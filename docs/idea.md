# Problocks Light — Project Idea

## Vision
A Roblox-like educational gaming platform. Think Khan Academy + ALEKS + Kahoot + Prodigy — but gamified like Roblox, built lightweight enough to run on low-end Chromebooks.

## Target Hardware
Dell Chromebook 11 3100:
- 1366 x 768 display
- Intel Celeron N4020
- 4 GB RAM
- 16 GB Flash Memory
- Chrome OS
- Intel HD Graphics

## Tech Approach
- WebAssembly-based for lightweight, cross-platform performance on Chromebooks
- AI-powered adaptive questions using Gemini 3.0 Flash Preview ($0.50/1M input tokens, $3/1M output tokens)

## How It Works

### For Teachers
- Each teacher codes/configures a game for their class
- Teachers can restrict students to only their assigned games (no distractions)
- Games played during last 15 minutes of class or once a week

### For Students
- **Grinding**: Students can continue playing/learning after class
- **Coin System**: Earn coins through gameplay/learning → buy skins, cosmetics
- **Skill Points**: Earn points that contribute to global rankings
- **Gear System**: Buy gear that provides in-game advantages
- **PvP**: Compete head-to-head with other students
- **Village Building**: Build villages/worlds with earned resources
- **Adaptive Learning**: AI generates questions based on how the student answered previous questions, identifies knowledge gaps and pushes on them (similar to ALEKS but AI-driven)

## Business Model

### Revenue Stream 1: School Subscriptions
- **$1 per student per year** — intentionally low to undercut competition (ALEKS, Khan Academy, etc.)
- Barely covers costs initially — this is a market penetration strategy

### Revenue Stream 2: Corporate Sponsorships (Primary Revenue)
- Companies sponsor the platform by having their names displayed in quizzes related to their industry
  - Math questions → Texas Instruments, IBM
  - Economics questions → Goldman Sachs, Walmart
  - Science questions → relevant STEM companies
- **No student data sold to companies** — only brand name placement/familiarity
- Value prop to sponsors: brand awareness with the next generation of consumers/employees

## Go-to-Market Strategy
- **First target**: Harmony Science Academy schools in Texas (existing close connections)
- $1/student/year is highly plausible for school budgets
- Students already play video games on Chromebooks — redirect that energy toward learning
- Prove the model with one district, then expand

## Competitive Advantage
- Much cheaper than ALEKS, Khan Academy Pro, IXL
- Gamification layer that competitors lack (Roblox-style engagement)
- AI-adaptive learning (not just static question banks)
- Runs on the cheapest Chromebooks schools already own
- Corporate sponsorship model keeps student costs near-zero
