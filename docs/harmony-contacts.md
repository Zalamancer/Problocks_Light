## Recommended Outreach Strategy

### Step 1: Use your existing connection
Ask them to intro you to **Pat Yongpradit (Chief Academic Officer)** or **Levent Sakar (Director of STEM Programs)**. These are the people who evaluate and champion educational tools.

### Step 2: Pitch to academic leadership
Pat Yongpradit or the curriculum team decides "is this good for students?" If they say yes, it moves up.

### Step 3: If you can get a board intro
**Dr. Oner Celepcikay (Board Chair)** is literally an AI Engineer at IBM who teaches Data Science at Rice. He would immediately understand adaptive AI-powered learning. A warm intro to him could accelerate everything.

### Step 4: CEO signs off
Fatih Ay has a Master's in instructional technology. He'll understand the tech. But he won't take a cold pitch — it needs to come recommended from his team or board.

### DO NOT cold-email principals or teachers first
That's bottom-up and slow. Harmony is a centralized charter network — decisions come from Houston central office.
